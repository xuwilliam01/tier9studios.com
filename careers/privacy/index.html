Applicant privacy notice

This covers what you send when you apply for a job at Tier 9 Game Studios Ltd. through this website. Our games have their own privacy policy.

What we collect

What you put in the application form: your name and email, your phone number and city if you give them, your resume, your links, your message, your answers to the role’s questions, and the time you agreed to this notice.

Our server uses your internet address briefly to limit abuse of the form. It is not saved with your application.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. What you type is kept in your browser tab until you send it, so a reload does not lose it.

Why

To consider you for the role and to contact you about it. We rely on your consent, which you give when you send the form. People read the applications; no automated tool scores or rejects them.

Who can see it

The people at Tier 9 responsible for hiring. We do not sell it or share it with other companies for their own use. It is stored on a server we run with a hosting provider, which may keep it outside Canada, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

We delete an application, including the resume, 12 months after the last activity on it: its arrival, a change of stage, or a note from the hiring team. If we hire you, it becomes part of your employment record. Backups made before a deletion can hold a copy until they expire.

Your choices

You can ask at any time for a copy of what we hold, for a correction, or for your application to be deleted. Write to autobattlesonline@gmail.com from the address you applied with, and include your reference code if you have it.

If you are unhappy with how we handle a request, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the privacy regulator where you live.

Who we are

Tier 9 Game Studios Ltd., Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
